WatchPilot Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026 • App Package: com.dpjha.moviebuddy • Contact: dpjha84@gmail.com

WatchPilot respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data WatchPilot collects, how it is used, and the controls you have over your data.

1. Information We Collect & Process

  • Account Information (Optional): If you sign in with Google, we receive your Firebase User ID, display name, and email address via Google Sign-In / Firebase Authentication to sync your watchlist, ratings, custom collections, episode progress, and Pro entitlement across devices. Guest Mode stores library data locally on your device.
  • User-Created Library & Preferences: Titles you track, ratings, reviews, custom lists, selected streaming subscriptions, and preferred region/language.
  • Live Swipe-to-Match Rooms: When you create or join a 6-digit Match Room, participant user IDs, room code, region, selected providers, and swipe choices are temporarily synced in Firebase Firestore to detect mutual matches.
  • Screenshot Vision Scanning (Snap-to-Watchlist): When you explicitly select a screenshot using the system Android Photo Picker, the selected image is sent via Firebase AI Logic to Gemini Vision solely to identify movie/TV titles and is not permanently retained by WatchPilot.
  • Usage & Diagnostics (Optional Toggle): Firebase Analytics records privacy-safe screen views, feature usage, and conversion events (you can disable analytics anytime in Profile).
  • Advertising & Purchases: WatchPilot Free displays Google AdMob ads (using device Advertising ID where permitted). WatchPilot Pro purchases and subscriptions are processed securely by Google Play Billing; WatchPilot does not collect or store payment card details.

2. Third-Party Services & Data Attribution

WatchPilot integrates the following SDKs and APIs:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Analytics, Firebase App Check, Firebase AI Logic)
  • Google AdMob (Banner, Interstitial, and Rewarded ads on the Free tier)
  • Google Play Billing (In-app purchases and subscriptions)
  • TMDB API & JustWatch: This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Streaming availability data is powered by JustWatch.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

You can export your library anytime in Profile, disable Analytics collection in Profile Settings, or permanently delete your account and all synced Cloud Firestore data immediately in Profile → Delete Account or by following our Account Deletion Guide.

4. Children's Privacy

WatchPilot is intended for general audiences aged 13 and older and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Contact

Questions or privacy requests? Email dpjha84@gmail.com.
