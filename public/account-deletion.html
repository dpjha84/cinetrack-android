WatchPilot Account & Data Deletion

App Package: com.dpjha.moviebuddy • Support Email: dpjha84@gmail.com

Instant Self-Service Deletion (In-App)

  1. Open WatchPilot on your Android device.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to the legal & account section and tap Delete Account → Delete Permanently.

This permanently deletes your Firebase Authentication account and all synced Cloud Firestore data (profile, tracked watchlist, ratings, custom collections, episode progress, favorite people, search history, and cloud preference records).

Web / Email Deletion Request (If App Uninstalled)

If you no longer have access to the app, send an email from your Google Sign-In address to dpjha84@gmail.com with the subject "WatchPilot Account Deletion Request". Requests are processed within 7 business days.

Data Retained or Handled Separately

  • Google Play Purchase History: Subscription/purchase receipts are managed by Google Play and retained by Google in accordance with tax and billing laws.
  • Local Device Data: Local Room cache on your device can be cleared anytime via Android App Settings or by uninstalling WatchPilot.
